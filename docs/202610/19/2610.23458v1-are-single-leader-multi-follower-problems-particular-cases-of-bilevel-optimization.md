## 摘要
二十多年来，单领导者多跟随者（SLMF）问题一直被视为比双层优化更难的一类问题。在这项工作中，我们对这一普遍看法提出了挑战，研究了SLMF问题是否可以重新表述为等价的双层优化问题。我们同时考虑了乐观和悲观的表述。对于乐观情况，答案是肯定的：通过将跟随者的均衡表示为单个优化问题解映射的不动点，我们推导出了一个等价的双层问题。对于悲观问题，情况则有所不同。我们证明了每一个乐观三层问题都承认一个具有两个跟随者的等价悲观SLMF表述。反之，对于每一个满足轻度可达性假设的悲观SLMF问题，我们建立了等价的乐观三层重新表述。这些重新表述简单、易于处理，并且保留了凸性和线性数据。因此，除非 $\text{NP}=Σ_2^p$，我们作为一个副产品推断出：多个跟随者在乐观方法和悲观方法之间引入了意料之外的复杂性差距，而这在仅考虑单个跟随者时是不存在的。

## Abstract
For more than 20 years, single-leader multi-follower (SLMF) problems have been treated as a more difficult class of problems in comparison to bilevel optimization. In this work we challenge this common belief, studying whether SLMF problems can be reformulated as equivalent bilevel optimization problems. We consider both optimistic and pessimistic formulations. For the optimistic case the answer is positive: We derive an equivalent bilevel problem by representing the followers' equilibria as fixed points of the solution mapping of a single optimization problem. For pessimistic problems, the situation is different. We show that every optimistic trilevel problem admits an equivalent pessimistic SLMF formulation with two followers. Conversely, for every pessimistic SLMF problem verifying a mild attainability assumption we establish an equivalent optimistic trilevel reformulation. The reformulations are easy, readily tractable, and they preserve convex and linear data. Thus, unless $\text{NP}=Σ_2^p$, we deduce as byproduct that multiple followers induce an unexpected complexity gap between optimistic and pessimistic approaches, which is not the case when considering one follower alone.
