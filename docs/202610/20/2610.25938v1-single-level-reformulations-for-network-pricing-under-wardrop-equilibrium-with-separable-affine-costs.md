## 摘要
我们研究了在拥堵网络的弧子集上设置通行费以最大化网络管理机构收益的问题，其中假设用户根据 Wardrop 均衡进行路径选择。该问题自然地可以表述为一个双层规划，其中领导者设定通行费，而跟随者通过选择均衡路径来进行响应。我们重点关注可分离仿射弧成本函数这一重要的特殊情况。在此假设下，Wardrop 均衡承认 Beckmann 的凸特征，由此我们利用 Beckmann 的公式及其对偶性，推导出了该双层问题的三种新的单层重构：基于弧的公式（我们对其进行精确求解）和两个基于路径的公式（通过列与约束生成法求解）。除了这些重构之外，我们还进一步从 Beckmann 势函数推导出了最优收益的上界，该上界可用于验证所有公式的间隙（optimality gaps）。对源自苏福尔斯（Sioux Falls）和马萨诸塞州东部网络实例的广泛计算研究，将我们的公式与 Beck、Labbé 和 Schmidt 最近提出的混合整数非线性公式进行了比较。基于弧的公式比后者能够为显著更多的实例求解出经证明的最优解（在 1800 秒的时间限制内），并且与后者不同，它在每个实例上都能返回可行解。它在最大的 74 节点网络上依然有效，而 Beck 等人的公式在该网络上无法求解任何实例。通行费上界的大小被证明是可解性的主要驱动因素：一旦取消对通行费的监管上限，基准测试中真正困难的核心就会显现出来。这两个基于路径的公式作为启发式方法使用时，在已知最优值的每个实例上都能返回与最优解相差百分之一以内的解，通常在精确公式达到时间限制的几秒钟内即可完成。

## Abstract
We consider the problem of setting tolls on a subset of the arcs of a congested network so as to maximize the revenue of a network authority, anticipating that users route according to a Wardrop equilibrium. This problem is naturally cast as a bilevel program in which the leader sets tolls while the followers respond by choosing an equilibrium routing.   We focus on the important special case of separable affine arc cost functions. Under this assumption, Wardrop equilibria admit Beckmann's convex characterization, from which we derive three new single-level reformulations of the bilevel problem, exploiting both Beckmann's formulation and its dual: an arc-based formulation, which we solve exactly, and two path-based formulations, solved by column-and-constraint generation. Beyond the reformulations, we further derive from Beckmann's potential an upper bound on the optimal revenue that can be used to certify optimality gaps across all formulations.   An extensive computational study on instances derived from the Sioux Falls and Eastern Massachusetts networks compares our formulations with the recent mixed-integer nonlinear formulation of Beck, Labbé and Schmidt. The arc-based formulation solves substantially more instances to proven optimality (within a time limit of 1800 s) than this formulation and, unlike it, returns a feasible solution on every instance. It remains effective on the largest, 74-node network, on which the formulation of Beck et al. solves none. The magnitude of the upper bounds on the tolls proves to be a major driver of tractability: the genuinely hard core of the benchmark emerges once the regulatory cap on tolls is removed. The two path-based formulations, used as heuristics, return a solution within one percent of the optimum on every instance whose optimal value is known, often in seconds where exact formulations reach the time limit.
