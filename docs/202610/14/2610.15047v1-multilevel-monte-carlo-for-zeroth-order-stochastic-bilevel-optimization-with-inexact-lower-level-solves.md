## 摘要
变分正则化技术在数学成像领域占主导地位。这些方法的表现力严重依赖于所采用的正则化公式，该公式通常包含许多需要用户微调的参数。为了避免与参数调优相关的大量计算成本，数据驱动的学习策略已被证明是一种有效的策略。通过从数据中学习来选择参数会产生一个分层（双层）优化问题，这通常在计算上很难解决。本文旨在为双层学习问题开发一种数据高效的求解策略。为此，我们提出了一种多水平蒙特卡洛无导数优化方法，该方法将上层目标（参数选择）的高斯平滑与底层（变分正则化）求解精度的层次结构结合起来。在不依赖底层问题具体求解方式的前提下，我们为近似求解整个双层学习问题推导出了明确的预言机复杂度（oracle complexity）和计算成本表述。我们的表述以所采用的底层求解方法为参数，从而实现了从变分重建方法到参数选择问题的直接成本核算。这使我们能够给出双层学习的通用端到端复杂度分析，该分析适用于反问题中最主要的优化常规，而不是针对特定方法的选择。

## Abstract
Variational regularization techniques are dominant in the field of mathematical imaging. The expressiveness of these methods heavily depends on the adopted regularization formulation which usually involves a number of parameters which have to be fine-tuned by the user. To circumvent the heavy computational costs associated with this parameter tuning, data-driven learning strategies have turned out to be a useful strategy. Selecting parameters by learning from data gives rise to a hierarchical (bilevel) optimization problem, which is in general computationally hard to solve. Our aim in this paper is to develop a data-efficient solution strategy for the bilevel learning problem. To that end, we propose a multilevel Monte Carlo derivative-free optimization method that couples Gaussian smoothing of the upper-level objective (parameter selection) with a hierarchy of lower-level (variational regularization) solution accuracies. Being agnostic towards the specific way the lower-level problem is solved, we derive explicit oracle complexity and computational cost statements for approximately solving the entire bilevel learning problem. Our statements are parameterized by the employed lower-level solution method, thus allowing for a direct cost accounting from the variational reconstruction method to the parameter selection problem. This enables us to give a universal end-to-end complexity analysis of bilevel learning, valid for the most important optimization routines in inverse problems, rather than for a specific choice of methods.
