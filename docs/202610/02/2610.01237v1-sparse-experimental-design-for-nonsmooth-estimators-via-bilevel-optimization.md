## 摘要
最优实验设计（OED）旨在决定获取哪些测量值以用于后续估计。经典方法通常通过优化源自线性高斯模型的信息准则来实现，该模型下的估计器具有闭式解。然而，许多现代估计器（如 Lasso、弹性网络或基于全变分的估计器）是非光滑的，且缺乏闭式解。为了将 OED 扩展到这些场景，我们将该问题重构为其本质形式：一个双层规划问题，其下层计算所部署的非光滑估计器，上层则对其验证预测风险进行评分。我们不再预先固定测量数量，而是为每个连续的采集权重设定一个凹稀疏价格。因此，保留多少测量值以及保留哪些测量值均成为优化的结果。只有当测量值的估计器感知价值超过其价格时，该测量才会被保留；通过逐步提高价格，可以得到一系列设计方案，从而探索测量基数与估计精度之间的权衡。利用价值函数惩罚重构，我们开发了一种单循环近端梯度算法，该算法避免了对非光滑解映射进行微分，并证明了其收敛至（近似）平稳点。在合成稀疏恢复和图像重建实验中，验证了所提方法的优势。

## Abstract
Optimal experimental design (OED) decides which measurements to acquire for downstream estimation. Classically, this is done by optimizing an information criterion derived from a linear-Gaussian model, for which the estimator is available in closed form. Many modern estimators such as Lasso, elastic net, or total-variation-based estimators, however, are nonsmooth and lack a closed-form solution. To extend OED to these scenarios, we recast the problem as what it implicitly is: a bilevel program whose lower level computes the deployed nonsmooth estimator and whose upper level scores its validation prediction risk. Rather than fixing the number of measurements in advance, we charge each continuous acquisition weight a concave sparsity price. As a result, how many and which measurements to keep are both outcomes of the optimization. A measurement survives only if its estimator-aware value exceeds its price, and progressively increasing the price yields a sequence of designs that explores the trade-off between measurement cardinality and estimation accuracy. Using a value-function penalty reformulation, we develop a single-loop proximal-gradient algorithm that avoids differentiating the nonsmooth solution map and establish its convergence to an (approximate) stationary point. Experiments on synthetic sparse recovery and image reconstruction demonstrate the benefits of the proposed method.
