## 摘要
受图像重建中参数辨识实验设计的启发，我们分析了一个在底层包含稀疏反演的双层控制问题。研究表明，在耦合系统的可观测性条件或由底层伴随变量诱导的接触集的勒贝格零测度（Lebesgue nullity）条件之一满足时，具有显式乘子形式的限定最优性条件成立。我们证明了这些条件彼此独立，并且在一般情况下不能同时省略。该方法基于与一组提升约束相关的局部一阶几何对象（切锥和法锥）的刻画。基于所得的最优性条件，我们提出了一种约化梯度法，并通过数值实验对其进行了说明。

## Abstract
Motivated by experiment design for parameter identification in image reconstruction, we analyze a bilevel control problem involving sparse inversion at the lower level. Qualified optimality conditions in explicit multiplier form are shown to hold under either an observability condition for a coupled system or the Lebesgue nullity of a contact set induced by the lower-level adjoint variable. We prove that these conditions are independent of one another and that they cannot, in general, both be omitted. The approach is based on the characterization of local first-order geometric objects (tangent and normal cones) associated with a set of lifted constraints. Based on the resulting optimality conditions, we formulate a reduced gradient method and illustrate it through numerical experiments.
