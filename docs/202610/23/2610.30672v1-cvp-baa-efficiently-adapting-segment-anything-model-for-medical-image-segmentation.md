## 摘要
各种特定任务的医学分割模型已表现出强劲的性能，但它们对大量注释和高度专业化架构的严重依赖，限制了其通用性、可扩展性和临床适用性。近期，适配视觉基础模型（特别是“分割一切模型”，即 SAM）已成为一种极具前景的替代方案。然而，主流的参数高效微调（PEFT）策略（如提示微调和适配器微调）通常缺乏明确的机制将局部结构先验嵌入到视觉Transformer（ViT）的长距离依赖建模中，导致它们对细粒度细节不够敏感。为了解决这些局限性，我们引入了一种新颖的混合 PEFT 方法——CVP-BAA，它提高了 SAM 对领域特定纹理和局部细节的敏感性，从而实现更优的边界勾勒。所提出的 CVP-BAA 采用了两个核心理念。首先，它引入了一种卷积视觉提示（CVP）策略，该策略利用卷积特征作为结构先验，将其前置到精心选择的 ViT 块中多头自注意力层的键和值投影中。通过专用的提示生成与注入模块，该机制有效地将局部空间细节与全局上下文语义连接起来。其次，设计了一个边界感知适配器（BAA），作为同一选定块中前馈网络的并行分支。通过显式计算和放大高频成分，BAA 增强了沿组织边界和细粒度病灶结构的特征表示。在三种成像模态的五个基准数据集上进行的广泛实验表明，CVP-BAA 实现了卓越的分割性能，在定量指标和定性结果上均超越了现有的特定任务模型以及基于 PEFT 的 SAM 适配方法。

## Abstract
Various task-specific medical segmentation models have demonstrated strong performance, their heavy reliance on extensive annotations and highly specialized architectures limits their versatility, scalability, and clinical applicability. Recently, adapting vision foundation models, particularly the segment anything model (SAM), has emerged as a promising alternative. However, prevalent parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) strategies, such as prompt tuning and adapter tuning, often lack an explicit mechanism to embed local structural priors into the long-range dependency modeling of vision transformers (ViT), rendering them insufficiently sensitive to fine-grained details. To address these limitations, we introduce CVP-BAA, a novel hybrid PEFT method that improves SAM's sensitivity to domain-specific textures and local details, leading to superior boundary delineation. The proposed CVP-BAA adopts two key ideas. First, it introduces a convolutional visual prompting (CVP) strategy that leverages convolutional features as structural priors, prepending them to the key and value projections within the multi-head self-attention layers of strategically selected ViT blocks. Through a dedicated prompt generation-and-injection module, this mechanism effectively bridges local spatial details with global context semantics. Second, a boundary-aware adapter (BAA) is designed as a parallel branch to the feed-forward network within the same selected blocks. By explicitly computing and amplifying high-frequency components, the BAA enhances feature representations along tissue boundaries and fine-grained lesion structures. Extensive experiments on five benchmarks across three imaging modalities demonstrate that CVP-BAA achieves superior segmentation performance, surpassing existing task-specific models and PEFT-based SAM adaptations in both quantitative metrics and qualitative results.
