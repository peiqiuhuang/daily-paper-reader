## 摘要
基于BERT的模型需要数千个带标签的样本进行训练，而像GPT-X这样更大的模型每类仅需1到5个样本。然而，这类模型在性能、成本和时间之间的权衡仍未得到充分探索。在这项工作中，我们将调查文本数据分类到16个类别中。我们重点关注针对该问题的性能、成本和耗时的实验方法与结果。我们对比了训练好的RoBERTa模型与（现成的）GPT-3.5模型在文本分类上的这些参数。基线实验表明，GPT-3.5对10,000条记录生成预测耗时6小时，而基于RoBERTa的模型在T4 GPU上仅需12分钟。为了确定GPT-3.5的最佳提示词方法，我们探讨了批处理提示（batch prompting）。我们对（1）批处理大小和（2）少样本采样进行了实验。我们发现最佳批处理大小为20，并辅以人工精选的少样本。实验表明，训练好的RoBERTa模型的F1分数（96%）远高于GPT-3.5模型（83%）。对于GPT-3.5而言，批处理提示有助于在不影响任务性能的情况下将推理成本降低约70%。我们发现最佳方法是RoBERTa模型与GPT-3.5模型的集成（F1分数：97.5%），其推理成本仅为独立使用GPT-3.5时的4.4%。

## Abstract
BERT-based models require thousands of labeled samples to train. Larger models like GPT-X require only 1-5 examples per class. However, performance, cost and time tradeoffs of such models remain under-explored. In this work, we classify survey text data into 16 classes. We focus on experimental approaches and results on performance, cost, and time taken for this problem. We compare these parameters of a trained RoBERTa-based model, and an (off-the-shelf) GPT-3.5 model for text classification. Baseline experiments show that the time taken by GPT-3.5 for generating predictions on 10,000 records is 6 hours, whereas a RoBERTa-based model takes only 12 mins on a T4 GPU. To identify the best prompting approach for GPT-3.5, we explore batch prompting. We experiment with (1) batch-size, and (2) few-shot sampling. We find the best batch size is 20 with human few-shot curation. Experiments show the F1 score of a trained RoBERTa-based model (96%) is much higher than a GPT-3.5 model (83%). In case of GPT-3.5, batch prompting is beneficial in lowering inference costs by ~70%, without hindering task performance. We find the best approach is an ensemble of a RoBERTa-based and GPT-3.5 model (F1: 97.5%) incurring only 4.4% of inference cost compared to a standalone GPT-3.5.
