## 摘要
视觉-语言-动作（VLA）微调在每一步将图像与动作配对，但通常仅提供任务级的语言指令，使得时刻间的视觉相关性保持隐式。我们引入了“眼动追踪监督的凝视提示”（eye-tracker-supervised gaze prompting），利用虚拟现实（VR）远程操作期间记录的凝视数据，为 VLA 微调提供帧级的视觉引导。在训练过程中，记录的凝视位置被渲染为机器人头戴式摄像机图像上的十字准星。在部署时，一个轻量级预测器根据近期图像和指令估计凝视位置，从而在无需眼动追踪器或更改策略架构的情况下提供相同类型的视觉提示。以 π_0 为基础，凝视提示在六项现实世界的双臂操作任务中将平均成功率从 26.3% 提升至 56.0%，且在单一策略训练所有六项任务时也观察到了性能提升。我们发布了 GazeMani 数据集，其中包含 1,200 条带有同步凝视数据的远程操作轨迹。

## Abstract
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) fine-tuning pairs images with actions at every step, yet typically provides only a task-level language instruction, leaving moment-to-moment visual relevance implicit. We introduce \emph{eye-tracker-supervised gaze prompting}, which uses gaze recorded during VR teleoperation to provide frame-level visual guidance for VLA fine-tuning. During training, recorded gaze locations are rendered as crosshairs on the robot's head-camera images. At deployment, a lightweight predictor estimates gaze locations from recent images and the instruction, supplying the same type of visual prompt without an eye tracker or changes to the policy architecture. Instantiated with $π_0$, gaze prompting increases mean success from $26.3\%$ to $56.0\%$ across six real-world bimanual manipulation tasks, with gains also observed when a single policy is trained on all six tasks. We release \textsc{GazeMani}, a dataset of $1{,}200$ teleoperated trajectories with synchronized gaze.
