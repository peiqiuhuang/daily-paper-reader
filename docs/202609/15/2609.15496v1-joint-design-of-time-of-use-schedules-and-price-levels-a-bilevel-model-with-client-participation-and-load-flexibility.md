## 摘要
本文研究了电力零售商在追求利润最大化时，如何联合设计分时电价（ToU）方案（包括将全天划分为不同的定价时段，以及为每个时段设定价格水平）。该模型考虑了具有负荷灵活性的客户，这些客户可能会拒绝零售商的报价而选择外部替代方案。我们将此问题建模为一个双层规划问题：在下层，每个客户在满足逐点约束和每日总用电量固定的前提下，调整其用电行为，并同时选择一个受二次正则项惩罚的参与水平。我们证明了客户的最优响应可以在对数线性时间内计算得出。我们推导了两种等价的单层重构模型：一种是通过随机梯度上升法求解的闭式连续模型（利用时段起始时间和持续时间的连续重参数化），另一种是通过专用求解器求解的混合整数非线性规划模型。随机梯度上升方案能够以可扩展的方式，几乎确定性地收敛到 Clarke 临界累积点。基于法国零售电力市场 5000 个模拟家庭的案例研究表明，在 100 个代表性客户的测试中，梯度法在逐点问题上与商业混合整数求解器的结果偏差在最优利润的 0.3% 以内，且运行速度快 2 到 8 倍。在所考虑的实例中，将无约束的逐点价格限制为可行的分时电价方案，会导致两时段电价的利润损失估计为 1.3%-13.8%，三时段电价的利润损失为 0.6%-6.6%。

## Abstract
We study the joint design of a time-of-use (ToU) tariff (the partition of the day into pricing blocks and the price level attached to each block) by a profit-maximizing electricity retailer facing flexible clients who may refuse the offer in favour of an outside option. The problem is modeled as a bilevel program in which each client, at the lower level, reshapes its consumption under pointwise bounds and a fixed daily energy total, and simultaneously chooses a participation level penalized by a quadratic regularizer. We prove the client's best response is computable in loglinear time. We derive two equivalent single-level reformulations: a closed-form continuous one tackled via stochastic gradient ascent (using a continuous reparametrization of block start times and durations) and a mixed-integer nonlinear program solved via dedicated solvers. The stochastic ascent scheme provides Clarke-critical accumulation points almost surely, in a scalable way. On a case study from the French retail electricity market with $5000$ simulated households, the gradient method matches a commercial mixed-integer solver on the pointwise problem to within $0.3\%$ of the optimal profit while running two to eight times faster on $100$ representative clients. Restricting unconstrained pointwise prices to admissible ToU schedules yields an estimated profit loss of $1.3\%$- $13.8\%$ for two-period tariffs and $0.6\%$-$6.6\%$ for three-period tariffs on the instances considered.
