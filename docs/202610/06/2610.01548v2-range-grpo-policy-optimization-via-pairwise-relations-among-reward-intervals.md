## 摘要
随着大型语言模型（LLMs）应用范围的扩大，后训练对于使其适应下游任务变得愈发重要。然而，获取可靠的监督信号成本依然高昂，特别是在缺乏参考答案或可执行验证器的领域。“LLM-as-a-Judge”（大模型作为裁判）为无标签响应提供了可扩展的伪奖励，但单一的分数无法明确表示奖励的不确定性。这促使我们将伪奖励表示为经过共形校准的奖励区间。我们提出了 Range-GRPO，这是一个结合了少量标注数据与无标签提示的半监督后训练框架。在组相对策略优化（GRPO）中，学习信号依赖于每个采样组内的相对奖励比较。本文提出的目标函数通过成对比较奖励区间，而非将其简化为点奖励，从而使区间的不确定性能够影响这些信号的大小和方向。我们的理论分析刻画了这种区别，并证明了当所有奖励区间退化为点时，该目标函数可恢复为标准的 GRPO 优势函数。实验结果表明，Range-GRPO 在所评估的半监督方法中实现了最高的分布内和分布外平均性能，且所需的训练资源更少。

## Abstract
As the use of large language models (LLMs) expands, post-training has become increasingly important for adapting them to downstream tasks. However, obtaining reliable supervision remains costly, especially in domains without reference answers or executable verifiers. LLM-as-a-Judge provides scalable pseudo-rewards for unlabeled responses, but a single point score does not explicitly represent reward uncertainty. This motivates representing pseudo-rewards as conformally calibrated reward ranges. We propose Range-GRPO, a semi-supervised post-training framework that combines limited labeled data with unlabeled prompts. In Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), learning signals depend on relative reward comparisons within each rollout group. The proposed objective compares reward ranges pairwise rather than reducing them to point rewards, allowing interval uncertainty to affect both the magnitude and direction of these signals. Our theoretical analysis characterizes this distinction and shows that the proposed objective recovers the Dr$.$GRPO advantage when all reward ranges collapse to points. Empirically, Range-GRPO achieves the highest in-distribution and out-of-distribution average performance among the evaluated semi-supervised methods while requiring fewer training resources.
