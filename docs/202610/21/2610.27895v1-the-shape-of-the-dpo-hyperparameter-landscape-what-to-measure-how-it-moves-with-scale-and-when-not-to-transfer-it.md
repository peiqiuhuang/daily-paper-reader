## 摘要
直接偏好优化（DPO）被广泛应用于语言模型的后训练，但其性能高度依赖于超参数的选择，例如学习率和参考策略正则化参数 $\beta$。为了更好地理解 DPO 的超参数曲面，我们在同一模型家族的不同模型规模上，使用密集的超参数网格进行了广泛的实验。这使我们能够分析最优 DPO 超参数随模型大小和更新步数的演变规律，其中中间检查点为加速超参数优化过程提供了廉价的信号。此外，通过在静态和动态基准上评估我们的模型，我们确定了哪些基准能为超参数选择提供最强的信号。最后，我们研究了各种先进的超参数优化策略在此设置下的表现。实验表明，最优学习率随模型规模而变化，且超参数无法跨训练数据集进行迁移。我们还发现，验证损失是超参数优化的一个较差代理指标，因为最小化验证损失的超参数与下游基准的最优超参数之间仅存在微弱的相关性。因此，DPO 超参数需要针对目标模型、数据集和评估目标进行专门调优。我们同时表明，短期的候选训练运行可以大幅降低这种选择的成本。我们发布了所有的训练配置和评估结果，以支持对选择质量和训练成本的可复现分析。

## Abstract
Direct preference optimisation (DPO) is widely used for language model post-training, but its performance depends strongly on the choice of its hyperparameters, such as the learning rate and the reference-policy regularisation parameter $β$. To better understand the DPO hyperparameter landscape, we conduct extensive experiments using a dense hyperparameter grid across different model scales from the same family. This allows us to analyse how the optimal DPO hyperparameters evolve with model size and the number of update steps, with intermediate checkpoints providing an inexpensive signal to accelerate the hyperparameter optimisation process. Furthermore, by evaluating our models on both static and dynamic benchmarks, we identify which benchmarks provide the strongest signal for hyperparameter selection. Finally, we investigate how different state-of-the-art hyperparameter optimisation strategies perform in this setting. Our experiments show that the optimal learning rate varies with model size and that hyperparameters are not transferable across training datasets. We also find that validation loss is a poor proxy for hyperparameter optimisation, as hyperparameters that minimize validation loss only weakly correlate with optimal hyperparameters for downstream benchmarks. Therefore, DPO hyperparameters need to be tuned specifically for the target model, dataset, and evaluation objective. We also show that short candidate training runs can substantially reduce the cost of this selection. We release all our training configurations and evaluation results to support reproducible analysis of selection quality and training cost.
