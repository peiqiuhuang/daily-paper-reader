## 摘要
持续音频深度伪造检测要求在学习新出现的深度伪造方法的同时，保持对先前遇到过的语音的辨别能力。现有的数据集增量评估同时改变了真实语音域和深度伪造机制，使得两者的影响难以区分。我们在相同的训练、开发和评估池上构建了五种任务组织，以在受控的样本预算下研究这些因素。我们提出的“真实锚定机制增量”（RAMI）协议反映了实际场景，即现有的真实语音提供了一种循环的混合域参考，而新的深度伪造机制则增量式地出现。我们进一步提出了 RF-Prompt，这是一种非对称的持续提示学习方法，它通过共享的真实提示保留可重用的真实语音知识，并通过具有正交残差的继承式伪造专家来扩展特定机制的知识。输入自适应软融合将积累的专家组合成固定数量的注入标记，而无需在推理时获取任务标识。在 RAMI 协议下，RF-Prompt 实现了 10.110% 的平均等错误率（EER）和 10.370% 的汇总 EER，优于所有评估的持续学习基线。在五种受控协议中，RAMI 均获得了最低的共同平均 EER 和汇总 EER。组件消融实验、有限数据实验以及跨骨干网络评估进一步验证了所提设计的有效性。

## Abstract
Continual audio deepfake detection requires learning newly emerging deepfake methods while retaining discrimination of previously encountered speech. Existing dataset-incremental evaluation changes both real-speech domains and deepfake mechanisms, making their effects difficult to distinguish. We construct five task organizations over identical training, development, and evaluation pools to study these factors under a controlled sample budget. Our proposed Real-Anchored Mechanism-Incremental (RAMI) protocol reflects the practical setting in which available real speech provides a recurring mixed-domain reference while new deepfake mechanisms arrive incrementally. We further propose RF-Prompt, an asymmetric continual prompt-learning method that preserves reusable real-speech knowledge through a shared real prompt and expands mechanism-specific knowledge through inherited fake experts with orthogonal residuals. Input-adaptive soft fusion combines the accumulated experts into a fixed number of injected tokens without requiring task identity at inference. On RAMI, RF-Prompt achieves 10.110% average EER and 10.370% pooled EER, outperforming all evaluated continual-learning baselines. Across the five controlled protocols, RAMI yields the lowest common-average and pooled EER. Component ablations, limited-data experiments, and cross-backbone evaluations further validate the proposed design.

### 2. 方法论：RF-Prompt
*   **核心思想**：采用非对称的提示学习（Prompt Learning）架构，将“真实语音知识”与“伪造机制知识”解耦。

    *   **伪造专家扩展（Fake Expert Expansion）**：为每种新机制引入一个“伪造专家”。新专家继承历史专家知识，并学习一个正交残差（Orthogonal Residual），通过正交正则化鼓励新知识向不同方向扩展。
    *   **输入自适应融合（Input-Adaptive Fusion）**：利用软混合专家（Soft MoE）机制，根据输入音频的特征动态加权融合所有已积累的伪造专家，无需在推理时提供任务 ID。
