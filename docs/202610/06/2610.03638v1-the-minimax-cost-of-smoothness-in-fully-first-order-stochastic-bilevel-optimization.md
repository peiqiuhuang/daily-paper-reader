## 摘要
我们研究了在非凸-强凸随机双层优化中，利用具有有界方差的新鲜、全局无偏一阶样本，寻找期望超梯度范数至多为 ε>0 的点的样本复杂度。对于低层变量平滑阶数 p≥1，F²SA-p 达到了 Õ(pε⁻⁴⁻²/ᵖ) (Chen et al., 2026)，但指数 4+2/p 的必要性此前尚不明确。我们在固定的非退化正则性预算下，证明了匹配的上下界 Θₚ(ε⁻⁴⁻²/ᵖ)，其中常数允许依赖于 p。该下界适用于固定样本预算下的无限制随机算法，允许维度随精度增加，并保持了全局无偏性及所有预设的低层变量平滑度界限，即使在标量低层变量和精确上层梯度的情况下依然成立。我们进一步刻画了当 Lⱼ=MΛʲ(j!)ᵝ (j≥3) 时的极小极大固定预算复杂度：Qₚ,ᵝ*(ε)=Θ(ε⁻⁴[inf₁≤r≤p,r∈ℕ rᵝε⁻¹/ʳ]²)，其中 p∈ℕ∪{∞}，且常数与 p 无关。在无穷阶情况下，当 β=0 时复杂度为 Θ(ε⁻⁴)，当 β=1 时为 Θ(ε⁻⁴log²(1/ε))。一种采用加权独立批次的梯度差分方法可以在所有阶数上一致地达到这些界限。因此，定量平滑性带来了精确的增益，而仅凭定性解析性仍允许 Θ(ε⁻⁶) 的复杂度。

## Abstract
We study the sample complexity of finding a point with expected hypergradient norm at most $ε>0$ in nonconvex--strongly-convex stochastic bilevel optimization using fresh, globally unbiased first-order samples with bounded variance. For lower-variable smoothness order $p\ge1$, F$^2$SA-$p$ achieves $\widetilde O(pε^{-4-2/p})$ (Chen et al., 2026), but necessity of the exponent $4+2/p$ was open. We prove matching upper and lower bounds $Θ_p(ε^{-4-2/p})$ under fixed nondegenerate regularity budgets, with constants allowed to depend on $p$. The lower bound holds for unrestricted randomized algorithms under a fixed sample budget, allows dimension to grow with accuracy, and preserves global unbiasedness and all prescribed lower-variable smoothness bounds, even with a scalar lower variable and exact upper gradients. We further characterize the minimax fixed-budget complexity under $L_j=MΛ^j(j!)^β$ for $j\ge3$: $Q_{p,β}^*(ε)=Θ\left(ε^{-4}\left[\inf_{1\le r\le p,,r\in\mathbb N} r^βε^{-1/r}\right]^2\right)$, for $p\in\mathbb N\cup{\infty}$, with constants independent of $p$. At infinite order, this gives $Θ(ε^{-4})$ for $β=0$ and $Θ(ε^{-4}\log^2(1/ε))$ for $β=1$. A gradient-difference method with weighted independent batches attains these bounds uniformly in order. Thus quantitative smoothness yields precise gains, whereas qualitative analyticity alone still permits $Θ(ε^{-6})$ complexity.
