## 摘要
开放词汇视听事件定位旨在为已见和未见的视听事件推断出明确的片段级类别。现有的解决方案严重依赖手工设计的提示模板，其预测结果对微妙的措辞变化高度敏感。可学习提示消除了这种手动调参的负担，但当仅在已见事件上进行训练时，它们往往会过拟合到已见类别，并扭曲泛化到未见类别所需的跨事件语义拓扑结构，我们将这种失效称为语义拓扑漂移。在本文中，我们提出了 SIMPLE，一个语义结构对齐的提示学习框架，它在异步音频和视觉流中对齐了可学习提示与手工提示之间的跨类别语义拓扑。具体而言，我们的 SIMPLE 将可学习连续提示微调（LCPT）与语义拓扑一致性正则化（STCR）相结合。LCPT 通过数据驱动的优化实现了提示工程的自动化，而 STCR 模块在随机类别子集采样下对齐可学习提示和手工提示的类别间相似度分布，以缓解语义拓扑漂移。在 OV-AVEBench 上的广泛实验和分析表明，我们的 SIMPLE 框架在已见和未见数据集划分上均实现了最先进的性能，同时仅使用了大约一半的可训练参数和约三分之一的训练时间。

## Abstract
Open-vocabulary audio-visual event localization aims to infer explicit segment-level categories for both seen and unseen audio-visual events. Existing solutions rely heavily on hand-crafted prompt templates, whose predictions are highly sensitive to subtle wording variations. Learnable prompts remove this manual tuning burden, but when trained solely on seen events, they tend to overfit to seen categories and distort the inter-event semantic topology required for generalization to unseen categories, a failure we term semantic topology drift. In this paper, we propose SIMPLE, a semantic structure-aligned prompt learning framework that aligns cross-category semantic topology between learnable and hand-crafted prompts across asynchronous audio and visual streams. Specifically, our SIMPLE combines Learnable Continuous Prompt Tuning (LCPT) with Semantic Topology Consistency Regularization (STCR). The LCPT automates prompt engineering through data-driven optimization, while the STCR module aligns the category-wise similarity distributions of learnable and hand-crafted prompts under random category subset sampling to alleviate semantic topology drift. Comprehensive experiments and analyses on the OV-AVEBench demonstrate that our SIMPLE framework achieves state-of-the-art performance on the seen and unseen splits, while using roughly half the trainable parameters and roughly one-third the training time.
