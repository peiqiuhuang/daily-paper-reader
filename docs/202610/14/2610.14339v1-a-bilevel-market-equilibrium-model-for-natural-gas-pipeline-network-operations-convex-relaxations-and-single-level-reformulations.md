## 摘要
我们提出了一个集成的市场均衡模型，用于确定天然气管道网络中的现货市场价格和流量。传统方法通常将市场均衡模型与天然气网络流量分配问题分阶段顺序求解。我们提出的框架通过双层优化公式将营销与运营结合起来，其中上层的市场均衡模型与下层的天然气网络流量模型相连接并同时求解。我们引入了针对非凸Weymouth管道压降方程的凸松弛方法，以及压缩机运行成本的替代模型。此外，我们基于Karush-Kuhn-Tucker（KKT）最优性条件、沃尔夫对偶性（Wolfe duality）和拉格朗日对偶条件，对该双层问题的多种单层重构方法进行了比较。对一个小型6节点网络的数值算例验证了我们模型的有效性和准确性，以及对不同基于对偶性和最优性的重构方法的比较。

## Abstract
We propose an integrated market equilibrium model for determining the spot market prices and flow rates in a natural gas pipeline network. The traditional approach solves the market equilibrium model separately from the natural gas network flow allocation problem in a sequential manner. Our proposed framework synthesizes marketing and operations by using a bilevel optimization formulation where the market equilibrium model in the upper level is connected and simultaneously solved with the natural gas network flow model in the lower level. We introduce convex relaxations for the nonconvex Weymouth pipe pressure drop equation and a surrogate for the compressor operating cost. Furthermore, we compare multiple single level reformulations of the bilevel problem based on Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) optimality conditions, Wolfe duality, and Lagrange duality conditions. Numerical studies on a small 6-node network demonstrates the efficacy and accuracy of our model and the comparison between different duality and optimality based reformulations.
