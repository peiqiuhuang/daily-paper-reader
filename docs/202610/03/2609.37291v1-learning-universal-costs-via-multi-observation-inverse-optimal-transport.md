## 摘要
经典的反向最优传输（IOT）通常从单一观测到的传输计划中推断代价函数，这在实践中可能对噪声和观测偏差较为敏感。为了解决这一局限性，我们提出了多观测反向最优传输（M-IOT）框架，该框架能够从多个传输计划中学习一个通用的代价函数。我们将由此产生的双层优化问题重构为一个可处理的单层凸规划问题，并刻画了最优解集的存在性及其几何结构。此外，我们提出了一种带有安德森外推法的块坐标下降（BCDwAE）算法，并证明了其 Q-线性收敛性。在合成数据、城市交通流和电子商务推荐系统上的大量实验表明，与单观测基准方法相比，M-IOT 降低了恢复误差，并能从含噪的多视图数据中提取出稳健的结构模式。

## Abstract
Classical Inverse Optimal Transport (IOT) typically infers a cost function from a single observed transport plan, which may be sensitive to noise and observational biases in practice. To address this limitation, we propose the Multi-Observation Inverse Optimal Transport (M-IOT) framework, which learns a universal cost function from multiple transport plans. We reformulate the resulting bilevel optimization problem into a tractable single-level convex program and characterize the existence and geometric structure of the optimal solution set. Moreover, we propose a Block Coordinate Descent with Anderson-type Extrapolation (BCDwAE) algorithm and establish its Q-linear convergence. Extensive experiments on synthetic data, urban mobility flows, and e-commerce recommendations demonstrate that M-IOT reduces recovery error compared to single-observation baselines and extracts robust structural patterns from noisy multi-view data.
